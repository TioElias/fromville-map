Overview
Located within the main Township, this two-story house is the family home of the Lius. As one of the most stable and tightly-knit family structures in the community, the house acts as a beacon of warmth, tradition, and sanctuary. It is protected by a Talisman at night, allowing the family a fragile sense of domestic normalcy amidst the surrounding terror.

Key Features
The Kitchen: The heart of the home, where Tian-Chen Liu routinely prepared home-cooked meals for her family and guests, keeping traditional cooking practices alive to preserve her family's heritage.

The Living Room: A communal space where Kenny, his mother, and his father gathered, trying to maintain a normal household routine despite the omnipresent threat outside their windows.

Notable Lore
The Burden of Dementia (Season 1): The home was a place of immense emotional strain before the Talismans were implemented. Kenny’s father, Bing-Qian Liu, suffered from advanced dementia, making it incredibly difficult for Kenny and Tian-Chen to keep him quiet and safe during the terrifying nights when they had to hide in underground root cellars.

A Sanctuary of Grief (Season 1): Following the brutal murder of Bing-Qian in the Clinic basement by the Creatures, the home shifted into a space of profound mourning for Kenny and his mother, testing their resilience and forcing Kenny to step up heavily as the Town's Deputy.

The Mother's Loss (Season 3): The house suffered its ultimate tragedy in Season 3 following the horrific execution of Tian-Chen Liu by the Creatures. Her absence leaves the home completely empty, leaving Kenny to navigate the house alone as a silent monument to the family he lost to the Town.
