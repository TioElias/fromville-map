Overview
Located within the main Township, this modest residential home belonged to the siblings Nathan and Sara Myers upon their arrival in Fromville. While it functions as a standard, Talisman-secured safe house at night, the home is a dark focal point in the town's history. It is where Sara's intense psychological torment began, ultimately transforming their sanctuary into the launching pad for a series of horrific internal betrayals.

Key Features
The Sibling Living Space: Before the town's peace was shattered, the house was a place where Nathan carefully looked after his deeply traumatized sister, trying to establish a quiet routine to help her cope with the reality of their entrapment.

The Living Room / Kitchen: The domestic setting where Sara first began experiencing the agonizing physical and auditory manifestations inflicted upon her by the mysterious entities of the forest.

Notable Lore
The Genesis of the Voices (Season 1): It was inside this home that Sara Myers first began hearing the distinct, intrusive voices of the town's unseen forces. The entities used her vulnerability to feed her information they shouldn't know, eventually forcing her into a state of psychological compliance under the promise that it would get everyone sent home.

The Death of Nathan (Season 1): The home became a scene of absolute tragedy after Sara succumbed to the voices' demands to kill Ethan Matthews. When Nathan discovered her dark intentions, a frantic struggle ensued inside the house. In his desperate attempt to stop his sister from becoming a monster, Nathan was accidentally killed when Sara inadvertently threw him into the path of a closing door/blade, stripping Sara of her only moral anchor.

An Abandoned Monument: Following Nathan's death and Sara's subsequent exposure, isolation, and eventual confinement in the church, the house stands as a chilling, semi-abandoned reminder to the rest of the Township of how easily the forces in the woods can corrupt the people inside the walls.
