Overview
Located far out in the deepest, uncharted parts of the forest, the Dungeon is a supernatural location that exists outside of Fromville's conventional space and time. It physically manifests as a crumbling, roofless stone tower or castle ruin. It is a place of immense suffering and ancient malice, serving as a literal prison where mysterious forces torture captives—both living and dead. It is accessible only through specific, supernatural gateways like the Farway Trees.

Key Features
The Stone Wall Shackles: Heavy iron chains bolted directly into the stone walls. These cuffs were used to permanently imprison captives, keeping them alive indefinitely through supernatural means despite severe starvation and dehydration.

The Invisible Rope Ladder: The only physical way out of the roofless structure. It cannot be seen from the ground, requiring someone from above to lower it down to anyone trapped inside the ruins.

The Music Box: A sinister, supernatural artifact that plays a haunting melody. It serves as the auditory anchor for the malevolent entity that controls the Dungeon, acting as a timer for its psychological torture.

Notable Lore
The Discovery of Martin (Season 2): After climbing through a Farway Tree, Boyd Stevens is instantly teleported inside the Dungeon. There, he finds Martin—a frail, elderly man chained to the wall who claims to have been trapped there for decades. Martin warns Boyd that the Creatures are just "the tip of the spear" and that there are far worse things hiding in the woods.

The Blood Infection: Before dying, Martin begs Boyd to kill him. When Boyd hesitates, Martin slashes Boyd's arm and passes a swarm of mysterious, parasitic worms under Boyd's skin, warning him that "my blood is your blood now." * The Ruin Paradox: When Boyd returns to the exact location later with his son Ellis, the intact stone tower is gone. In its place are only ancient, decayed stone ruins that look centuries old, proving the Dungeon shifts its appearance or exists in a completely different time period.

The Cicada Curse: The Dungeon acts as the epicenter of a nightmare curse. When the Music Box plays, a swarm of supernatural cicadas is unleashed into the Town. The insects burrow into residents like Paula and Reggie, killing them from the inside out or trapping their minds back inside the Dungeon's torture chamber. Boyd eventually destroys the entity by returning to the ruins and smashing the physical Music Box.
