### **Overview**
Located on the edges of the Township, this abandoned, rusted-out commercial cargo truck serves as Victor’s deeply private, off-the-grid hideout. While it appears to be mere junk to the rest of the Town, the interior has been entirely retrofitted by Victor over decades of isolation. Because it is small, inconspicuous, and heavily secured with a Talisman at night, it acts as his primary sanctuary where he preserves his childhood belongings, manages his trauma, and hides when the pressures of the community become too overwhelming.

---

### **Key Features**

* **The Cargo Hold Sanctuary:** The enclosed back of the truck, completely packed with Victor’s personal artifacts from the late 1970s, including old blankets, vintage lunchboxes, and childhood relics.
* **The Drawing Archives:** The interior walls are heavily covered with Victor’s crayon drawings, mapping out past events, tragedies, and changing geographical anomalies of the forest.
* **The Peach Can Stash:** A designated storage corner inside the truck where Victor hoards his ultimate comfort food—the town's rare, remaining cans of peaches.

---

### **Notable Lore**

* **The Safe Space for Trauma:** For decades, this truck has been the only place where Victor feels entirely in control of his environment. Having survived the original town-wide massacre alone as a child, the cargo bed acts as a psychological fortress where he can shut out the horrors of the outside world.
* **The Bond with Ethan (Season 1):** Victor brings Ethan Matthews to this hideout, introducing the young boy to his drawings and his collection of toys. It is here that Victor shares his unique perspective on the town’s "Quest," treating Ethan as an intellectual equal who can also see the Boy in White.
* **The Defense of Privacy:** Victor is fiercely protective of this space. As revealed through his interactions with Jade and Tabitha, he experiences severe anxiety and bouts of anger when other residents attempt to go through his things or invade his sanctuary, as it represents the final connection to his lost family.
