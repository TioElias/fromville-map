Overview
Located directly adjacent to the main grounds of Colony House, this small, secure outdoor shed is used by Donna Raines and her residents to store heavy equipment, tools, and communal supplies. Following the psychological strain and escalating conflicts among the survivors in Season 2, it is strictly repurposed as a secure lockup for firearms to keep weapons out of the communal house.

Key Features
The Weapons Locker: A designated, locked space inside the shed where Colony House guards store all firearms and ammunition away from the general populace.

The Vulnerable Threshold: While small enough to be protected by a Talisman, its physical distance from the main building makes walking to it a tense, high-stakes task when tensions are high or night is falling.

Notable Lore (Season 2)
The Firearms Lockup: Following a chaotic and sleepless night plagued by deaths and paranoia, Donna orders the guard Matthias to gather all the firearms from the main house and permanently lock them in the shed, stating she does not want guns circulating among the panicked residents.

The Murder of Matthias: While Matthias is finishing up inside the shed, a deeply unhinged Reggie approaches him under the guise of helping. Driven by madness and grief over the loss of his wife Paula, Reggie violently cuts Matthias' throat inside the shed, killing him instantly to steal a shotgun and wage his own war against the Town.
