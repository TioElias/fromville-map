Overview
Located deep within the uncharted woods near a large lake, the Log Cabins are a collection of ancient, decaying wooden structures that completely predate the modern 1950s-era Township. This area is highly atmospheric, heavily overgrown, and serves as a vital but deeply unnerving foraging site for the townspeople due to the wild crops that grow nearby.

Key Features
The Primitive Cabins: A cluster of archaic wooden huts built centuries ago. The architecture suggests that previous generations of trapped travelers attempted to establish a permanent colony here long before the current Town existed.

The Totem Perimeter: The cabins are surrounded by strange, hand-crafted wooden totems. Jade Herrera theorizes that these totems acted as a primitive, early version of the Talismans, used by the old settlers to keep the Creatures away at night.

The Wild Root Fields: The ground surrounding the cabins is one of the few places where crops like potatoes and cabbage grow naturally, making it a necessary destination when the Town's food supplies run low.

Notable Lore
Tabitha’s Dream Connection (Season 1): Upon seeing the cabins, Tabitha Matthews realizes they match a vivid nightmare she had as a child, long before her family ever crossed paths with the tree in the road. This drops the first major hint that the entities running the area can reach out across time and space.

The Iron Spike Vision (Season 2): While exploring the cabins, Jade Herrera is assaulted by a terrifying, localized vision of a man dressed in 1860s-era clothing pinned to the ground with a massive iron spike driven straight through his eye.

The Hidden Bear Trap (Season 2): The surrounding brush hides physical dangers left behind by past eras. During a foraging run, Kristi Miller steps into an ancient, rusted bear trap, forcing Kenny and Jade to perform a tense, grueling emergency extraction to save her leg before nightfall.
