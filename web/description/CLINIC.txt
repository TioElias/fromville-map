Repurposed from the Town’s former school building, the Clinic functions as Fromville's sole medical facility. Medical supplies are severely scarce, with the vast majority being painstakingly stripped or salvaged from an ambulance—affectionately nicknamed "T.R.O.Y."—that became permanently trapped in the Township. The main floor features makeshift cots for patients, while the secure basement is used for high-risk patients. It also serves as the personal living quarters for Kristi Miller, the third-year medical student acting as the Town's doctor.

Key Features
The Ambulance ("T.R.O.Y."): Permanently parked directly outside the Clinic, this Type II van-ambulance is the primary lifeblood of the town's medical operation, acting as the original source for critical supplies, medicine, and saline.

The Secure Basement: A lower storage area that initially provided a safe, enclosed nightly refuge for vulnerable residents before becoming a site of absolute horror.

Notable Lore
The Basement Massacre (Season 1): The Clinic became the scene of a devastating tragedy when Sara Myers suffered a psychological break under the influence of the voices. Sara intentionally left the building's door open at night, letting the Creatures inside. The monsters brutally eviscerated Nurse Gina and Kenny's father, Bing-Qian Liu, shattering the town's fragile sense of security.

The Autopsy of a Monster (Season 2): Following the death of the "Smiley" Creature, its corpse was brought to the Clinic. Operating under extreme dread, Kristi, Kenny, and Boyd performed an unprecedented autopsy on the monster, uncovering that its internal organs were completely withered and dried—save for a mysterious, toxic fluid.

The Reunion (Season 2): The building bridges the world before and the nightmare of the Town when a passenger bus arrives, unexpectedly carrying Kristi’s fiancée, Marielle (a pediatric nurse), who joins the Clinic staff.
