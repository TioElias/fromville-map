Overview
The Bottle Tree is a unique and highly significant variation of the forest's Farway Trees. Located in a secluded clearing, this large tree is easily identifiable by the dozens of glass bottles hanging from its branches by strings. Unlike most Farway Trees, which teleport travelers to completely random coordinates in the woods, the Bottle Tree acts as a stable, fixed portal—always sending whoever enters it to one specific, highly critical destination.

Key Features
The Hanging Bottles: The tree’s branches are heavily draped in glass bottles. Inside these bottles are rolled-up scraps of paper containing written numbers (such as "1864"). Victor’s mother believed these numbers represented the specific years that previous groups of people became trapped in Fromville.

The Fixed Anchor: While standard Farway Trees are volatile and unpredictable, the Bottle Tree serves as a reliable, direct highway to the edge of the Township's boundaries.

Notable Lore
The Lighthouse Gateway (Season 2): When Tabitha Matthews is desperate to find a way to save the "children in the tower," Victor leads her directly to this tree. He reveals that this was the exact location where his mother went on the night of the town massacre. Tabitha climbs into the hollow trunk and is instantly teleported to the base of the mysterious Lighthouse, proving the tree acts as a direct bridge to the tower.

The Mother's Artifact (Season 3): The area surrounding the Bottle Tree holds deep emotional trauma for Victor. It is near this clearing that Victor’s father, Henry, later discovers an old, rusted bicycle belonging to Victor's sister, Eloise—confirming she had chased their mother to this very spot on the night she died.

The Cycle Tracker: The years written on the papers inside the bottles serve as the primary piece of evidence that Fromville operates in distinct, historical cycles, dating back centuries before the current 1950s-era Town was built.
