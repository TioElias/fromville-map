Overview
Located within the main Township, this two-story house served as the primary residence for the Matthews family after they completed the Choosing Ceremony. Originally structured to offer the newly arrived family a sense of stability and individual privacy, the house became a central hub for engineering escape plans. However, its structural integrity was completely tied to the town's supernatural forces, leading to its catastrophic destruction in Season 2.

Key Features
The Dig Site / Basement: The most critical area of the house. Driven by a desperate need to find out where the town's impossible electricity comes from, Tabitha and Jim dug a massive hole through the basement floor, eventually breaching into the subterranean network where the Creatures sleep.

Ethan’s Bedroom: A space upstairs where Ethan mapped out his theories regarding the "Quest" and interacting with his visions of the Boy in White.

Notable Lore
The Family Divide (Season 1): Upon moving in, the house immediately reflected the trauma of the Choosing Ceremony. While Jim, Tabitha, and Ethan inhabited the home, their teenage daughter Julie chose to live at Colony House, fracturing the family unit from their very first day in Town.

The Basement Breach (Season 1): Following days of excavation, Tabitha finally broke through the basement floor, only to have the ground collapse beneath her. She fell directly into the Cave Entry/Tunnels, forcing Victor to rescue her from the dormant monsters while Jim desperately tried to dig her out from above.

The Catastrophic Collapse (Season 2): In a direct supernatural retaliation against Jim and Tabitha's attempts to investigate the town's infrastructure (via the radio tower and the basement hole), the entire house structurally failed. The building collapsed into a massive pile of rubble, trapping Jim and a newly arrived bus passenger, Brick, beneath the debris and killing Brick in the process.
