Overview
The designated worship building in the Town, featuring a main floor for sermons and a basement used for storage. Directly adjacent to the building is the Town's graveyard. Following the death of Father Khatri, the Church has evolved from a place of spiritual guidance into a somber space where the dead are brought, prepared for burial, and laid to rest.

Key Features
The Graveyard: The adjacent resting place where victims of the Creatures—including Lauren, Meagan Pratt, Nathan, and Tian-Chen Liu—are buried during formal funeral services.

The Storage Basement: A critical narrative flashpoint. It is used by Father Khatri to secretly hide and interrogate Sara Myers after her crimes, and later serves as her temporary shelter and a confrontation site for suspicious residents.

Notable Lore
The Unwritten 74th Book (Season 1): Inside the basement, Father Khatri shares his theory with Sara that the Town's residents are living through a dark, unwritten 74th book of the Bible, suggesting they were chosen for a specific purpose.

The Sins of the Town (Seasons 1 & 2): The Church is where Boyd and Khatri fiercely debate the ethics of "The Box" punishment. Later, an exasperated Boyd uses the empty Church to scream at God, demanding to know if their suffering is just a joke.

Preparation of the Dead (Season 3): Following the brutal murder of Tian-Chen Liu, Jade and Kristi use the Church to meticulously clean and prepare her body, granting her dignity before the community holds her funeral.

The Boy in White's Message (Season 3): Victor reveals that as a child, he entered the Church basement and witnessed the Boy in White speaking to Christopher. The Boy delivered a cryptic message: "The answers to the end are in the beginning," explaining that murdered children lay on stones where roots became Farway Trees. This revelation is what originally drove Victor's mother to leave him and look for the tree.
