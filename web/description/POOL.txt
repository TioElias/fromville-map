Overview
The Star Magic Motel Pool is a bizarre, unfinished landmark on the edge of the Township. While the inground concrete swimming pool and its accompanying "Star Magic Motel" sign were completely built, the actual motel building itself was never constructed. The empty, dry pool is unused by the residents and contains an overturned truck at the bottom—a vehicle that Boyd recalls belonged to former residents named Danny and Shay.

Key Features
The Motel Sign: A standalone vintage sign marking a motel that doesn't exist, serving as a strange piece of evidence regarding how the Town's impossible infrastructure was constructed.

The Overturned Truck: A rusted vehicle resting inside the dry pool basin, serving as a grim visual anchor that triggers Boyd's anxieties about the people he has lost over the years.

The Concrete Wall Cairn: A makeshift, solemn memorial erected out of stones inside the pool to cover up a horrific structural anomaly.

Notable Lore (Season 3)
Henry's Observation: Upon arriving in the Township, Victor's father, Henry, stands by the sign and points out the eerie contradiction of the location to Ethan Matthews: there is a sign and a pool, but absolutely no motel.

The Bottle Tree Disaster: The pool becomes the site of a gruesome supernatural accident when a resident named Dale panics and steps through the Bottle Tree. Instead of safely teleporting to the Lighthouse, the portal’s physics glitch, fusing Dale directly inside the solid concrete wall of the motel pool.

The Mercy Killing Attempt: As Dale is slowly crushed and suffocated to death inside the solid concrete, a devastated Boyd prepares to shoot him to end his misery, though Dale succumbs to his injuries just before the trigger is pulled. Boyd uses this horrific sight to warn the rest of the townspeople about the extreme dangers of blindly messing with the Farway Trees.

Donna's Memorial: Unable to chip Dale's body out of the structure, Donna Raines uses heavy stones to build a cairn directly over his exposed remains inside the concrete wall, transforming the unfinished pool into a permanent graveyard.
