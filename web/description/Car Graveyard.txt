Overview
A somber, sprawling clearing filled with the rusted remains of vehicles belonging to the Township's former residents. While it looks like an ordinary junk pile, it is actually a monument to the town's tragic history. After the Creatures wiped out the original population when he was a child, Victor painstakingly moved every car here so he wouldn't have to look at them while walking the empty streets.

Key Features
The Station Wagon: Victor’s mother’s old vehicle, which serves as a makeshift safe box where Victor hid his sister Eloise's childhood drawings.

The Rock Line: A distinct geographical boundary in the clearing. The vehicles parked beyond these rocks predate Victor’s arrival in the 1970s, proving the town's trap has been active for generations.

Christopher’s Car: The specific vehicle belonging to a former resident who went mad after obsessed with the mysterious symbols, eventually leading to the town's mass slaughter.

Notable Lore
The Origin of the Symbols: Victor brings Jade here to fulfill a bargain, revealing that the town's descent into madness began when Christopher started seeing the symbols and stopped smiling.

The Lighthouse Revelation: While sorting through the drawings in the trunk with Tabitha, Victor recovers his deeply repressed memories of his sister, Eloise, and uncovers a drawing of a Lighthouse—the place their mother died trying to reach.

A Sanctuary for Grief: In Season 3, overwhelmed by the impossible arrival of his father, Henry, Victor retreats to this graveyard to hide and process the trauma of his past before being comforted by Sara.
