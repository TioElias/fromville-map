Overview
Farway Trees are massive, ancient trees scattered throughout the forest that defy the laws of physics. They function as unpredictable, localized teleportation portals. When an object or a person is placed inside the hollow opening of a Farway Tree’s trunk, they are instantly transported to another location within the Fromville landscape.

Key Features
The Hollow Trunk: The physical gateway. Anything dropped or pushed into the opening disappears completely, re-emerging somewhere else.

Random Teleportation: Traveling through a Farway Tree is highly volatile. Unlike a standard door, a tree typically sends travelers to a completely random coordinate in the woods—with a few rare exceptions.

The "Through the Looking Glass" Physics: Items dropped down the tree fall upwards or outwards at their destination, operating on a logic that Victor describes as being similar to a fairy tale.

Notable Lore
Victor and Ethan's Expedition (Season 1): Victor introduces a fascinated Ethan Matthews to the tree's anomalous properties. To demonstrate how it works, Victor drops a rock into the trunk, and they watch it instantly drop from the sky a short distance away. Victor explains that while most destinations are random, some trees can have fixed endpoints if you know how to use them.

Boyd's Desperate Escape (Season 2): While trapped in the forest at night with Sara Myers as the Creatures close in, Sara pushes Boyd into a Farway Tree to save his life. The tree teleports Boyd directly inside the narrow, claustrophobic stone shaft of The Dungeon, where he discovers Martin.

Tabitha’s Descent (Season 2): Guided by Victor's memories of his mother's final night, Tabitha Matthews uses a specific Farway Tree to teleport directly to the base of the mysterious Lighthouse in an attempt to save the children trapped in the tower.
