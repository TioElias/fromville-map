### **Overview**
The Sheriff’s Office (historically the Town’s post office) serves as the absolute command center for law, order, and strategic defense in Fromville. Managed by Sheriff Boyd Stevens and Deputy Kenny Liu, this highly fortified building houses the town's administrative files, a temporary holding cell, and Boyd’s personal office. It is a vital sanctuary secured by a Talisman, frequently used to shelter displaced residents, contain dangerous secrets, and coordinate town-wide emergencies.

---

### **Key Features**

* **The Map of Arrivals:** Located inside the office, this is a master map pinned with the origins and roads of every resident trapped in the Town, serving as a bleak reminder of their shared, impossible geography[cite: 29, 30].
* **The "Nights Without Incident" Sign:** A prominent chalkboard outside the station used to track the town's days of safety. Following devastating events, the count is manually reset to zero or one, reflecting the fragile state of peace[cite: 20].
* **The Holding Cell:** A makeshift jail space used to lock up unruly residents or temporarily house survivors when other safe houses collapse[cite: 16, 18].
* **Boyd's Office Desk:** A central hub where critical supplies—including an extra revolver kept in the drawer and Father Khatri’s personal stash of alcohol—are stored[cite: 70, 86, 87].

### **Notable Lore by Season**

#### **Season 1: Tragedy and Transition**
* **The Death of Father Khatri:** Following a chaotic breach at the clinic, Boyd brings a fatally wounded Father Khatri to the steps of the station[cite: 11, 13]. Khatri tragically dies outside the doors while he and Boyd recite the Lord's Prayer[cite: 12, 15].
* **The Colony House Refuge:** After a devastating creature attack at Colony House, the station becomes a crowded temporary shelter[cite: 16]. The psychological toll peaks when a resident named Eric commits suicide inside the station overnight, resetting the town's safety sign to day "1"[cite: 20, 21].

#### **Season 2: Psychological Breaking Points**
* **The Bus Arrival Siege:** During the chaotic night the Grand Rapids bus arrives, Ellis and Fatima seek shelter inside the station[cite: 34, 35]. From the windows, they are forced to watch helplessly as stranded passengers outside are slaughtered by the Creatures[cite: 37, 38, 39].
* **Sara’s Russian Roulette:** Confined to the station, a guilt-ridden Sara Myers faces an aggressive confrontation with Kenny over her role in his father’s murder[cite: 57, 65, 67, 68]. In a moment of extreme psychological distress, Sara loads two bullets into Boyd's gun, points it at Kenny, and then pulls the trigger against her own head in a desperate game of chance[cite: 70, 74, 75].

#### **Season 3: Fractured Leadership and Dark Truths**
* **The Livestock Rescue:** Following the attack on the Barn, an injured Boyd uses his office to recover and argue with Donna over his controversial choice to risk human lives to save the town's wandering livestock[cite: 78, 89, 90, 91].
* **Officer Acosta's Disarming:** A panicked, newly arrived police officer named Acosta ransacks the office looking for her weapon[cite: 95, 102]. Boyd fiercely asserts his authority, returning her gun only after completely unloading the bullets, proving that normal badges mean nothing in the Township[cite: 105, 106, 108].
* **Boyd's Parkinson's Confession:** After experiencing severe tremors while searching for a missing Fatima, a breaking Boyd forces his son Ellis to sit down for a drink[cite: 114, 115, 117]. He finally confesses his secret diagnosis of Parkinson's disease, which he had been hiding since before his first trek into the forest[cite: 118].
* **The Taunting Voices:** While poring over maps to find Fatima, Sara arrives at the office with a terrifying message from the entities: the voices have returned, taunting Boyd that Fatima is close but he will be too late to save her[cite: 128, 129, 130, 131].
