Overview
Colony House is a grand, three-story historic mansion situated on a hill overlooking the Township. In direct ideological opposition to the individualist family structures of the Town, Colony House functions as a free-spirited, communal sanctuary. Led by Donna Raines, the residents share all resources, sleep in communal wards, grow their own food, and live by a philosophy of 'living in the present moment.' It is heavily secured with a Talisman, allowing the large group of survivors to sleep safely at night—until its defenses are catastrophically breached.

Key Features
The Greenhouse: Located on the mansion's grounds, this glass dome serves as the primary cultivation hub for the community's crops, representing their hope for sustainable survival.

The Communal Ward: The large dormitory rooms where dozens of residents sleep side-by-side, reflecting the house's radical rejection of traditional nuclear family units.

The Foyer and Grand Staircase: The central architectural space of the mansion, frequently used for communal dining, meetings, and parties.

Notable Lore
The Choosing Ceremony: Colony House is one of the two core factions that newcomers must choose between at the Choosing Stone. It offers a communal lifestyle of shared resources, whereas the Town offers individual family homes and a structured, rule-bound community.

The Season 1 Breach: The mansion becomes the site of one of the show's most horrific tragedies. A resident named Kevin becomes infatuated with a female Creature named Jasmine, opening a second-story window to let her in. Jasmine brutally eviscerates Kevin, opening the doors for a horde of monsters to enter. The resulting massacre claims the lives of numerous residents, forcing Donna, Boyd, and others to make a chaotic and bloody retreat to the Town center.

Fatima and Ellis's Wedding: Colony House is a place of rare joy in Season 2 when Fatima and Ellis (Boyd's son) are officially wed on the grand porch, representing the community's refusal to let the darkness destroy their humanity.
