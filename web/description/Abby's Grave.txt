Abby’s Grave
Abby’s Grave is a haunting, secluded landmark in the world of FROM, serving as a central point of grief and mystery for the Stevens family. Unlike the other residents of the town who are buried in the communal graveyard, Abby is interred in a remote clearing deep within the woods. The site is marked by a simple, weathered wooden cross, often adorned with fresh wildflowers left by her husband, Boyd, who visits to "talk" to her during moments of extreme crisis.

Architecturally, the location is significant due to its proximity to a prehistoric Stone Circle—a "Stonehenge-like" arrangement of ancient slabs that suggests the site may hold spiritual or supernatural power beyond just a final resting place.
