Overview
Hidden deep within the forest brush, this rocky opening is the primary gateway to the Tunnels—a sprawling, subterranean labyrinth directly beneath Fromville. Unbeknownst to the town's residents for years, this pitch-black network is where the Creatures sleep during the day. Entering the cave before nightfall is a death wish, as it exposes the intruder directly to the monsters in their dormant state.

Key Features
The Cave Paintings: The walls surrounding the entry passages are covered in eerie, primitive drawings. Victor reveals that these paintings were actually created by the Creatures themselves, hinting at a dark, ancient history.

The Hoarded Artifacts: Deep past the threshold lies a disturbing graveyard of human belongings. The Creatures don't just sleep here; they hoard the souvenirs of their victims, including suitcases, clothing, and old toys like the creepy ventriloquist dummy.

The Root Symbol: In the deepest chambers, the overhead tree roots intertwine to form the exact mysterious symbol that haunts Jade, acting as a natural skylight into the cave.

Notable Lore
The Basement Collapse (Season 1): Tabitha Matthews literally drops into this location after digging through her basement floor. She is rescued by Victor, who was instructed by the Boy in White to wait for her at the cave's edge and guide her safely past the sleeping monsters.

The "Anghkooey" Kids: The mouth of the cave acts as a spiritual hotspot. Tabitha is swarmed here by the spirits of the Ghoulish Children chanting "Anghkooey," a vision that triggers a severe physical toll (a bloody nose) and pushes her to find the Lighthouse.

Jade’s Spool of Thread (Season 2): Determined to decode his visions, Jade hitches a spool of thread to a tree outside the Cave Entry, mapping his way into the heart of the darkness. It is here that he witnesses the Ghoulish Children on stone altars beneath the root symbol.
