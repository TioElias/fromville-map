The Brundles is a small, scenic lake located near Colony House. It is the primary recreational spot for the town, characterized by a small wooden dock and a collection of toys left there by residents. The name "The Brundles" was given to the lake by the inhabitants of Colony House long before most of the current characters arrived.

Factual Events at the Brundles (Season 1-3)
Julie’s Introduction: Fatima uses the lake to help Julie adjust to the town, emphasizing that the community tries to maintain a sense of beauty despite the horror.

Elgin’s Arrival: Elgin’s first request in town is to see the water, confirming his connection to the "dream" he had on the bus.

The "Lake of Tears" Theory: While Ethan and Elgin both discuss a "Lake of Tears" from stories and visions, it has not been confirmed in the show that the Brundles is that lake. Many characters (and fans) suspect a connection, but as of the most recent episodes, the Brundles remains a relatively "quiet" location compared to the Barn or the Clinic.
