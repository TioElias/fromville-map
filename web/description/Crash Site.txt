Overview
Located on the main road leading into the Township, this is the exact intersection where the modern timeline of Fromville was shattered. It is the site of the unprecedented double-crash involving the Matthews' RV and Jade luxury car. In the history of the Town, two new vehicles arriving on the exact same day was an unheard-of anomaly—a catastrophic event that shattered the town's long-standing period of stability and triggered a dangerous new shift in the environment.

Key Features
The Matthews' RV: A large recreational vehicle that served as the temporary, highly vulnerable safe house for the Matthews family on their terrifying first night.

The Impaled Car: Jade's overturned sports car, which bore the brunt of the initial collision and left its passengers severely injured.

Notable Lore
The Two-Car Omen (Season 1): According to the town's lore—and later confirmed by the voices to Sara—the simultaneous arrival of two separate cars is a historic omen. The last time two cars arrived on the same day, it resulted in a mass slaughter where almost every resident in the Township was wiped out.

The First Night Siege: Following the crash, Ethan Matthews was left pinned inside the RV by a metal bracket through his leg, making it impossible to move him before sundown. Sheriff Boyd Stevens stayed behind, pinning a Talisman to the RV's window. This marked the first time a Talisman was successfully tested and proven to work inside a mobile vehicle, protecting the family while the Creatures circled outside all night.

The Casualties: While Jim, Tabitha, Julie, Ethan, and Jade survived the crash and the subsequent night, Jade's friend Tobey was taken to the clinic with severe injuries, where he was ultimately murdered by a compromised Sara before he could fully process the town's reality.
