Overview
A classic, mid-century American chrome and teal diner located at the absolute center of the Town. More than just a restaurant, the Diner acts as the literal heart of the community. It is where Fromville's residents gather to eat, start, and end their days together with communal meals. Because of its central location and large size, it functions as the town's primary food depository, as well as a storeroom for keeping the left-behind personal belongings of deceased residents.

Key Features
The Counter & Kitchen: Long a symbol of comfort, this area was fiercely managed by Tian-Chen Liu, who poured her heart into feeding the community until her tragic death in Season 3. Following her loss, the bus driver, Bakta, stepped in to cook and manage the daily operations.

The Tabletop Jukebox: A piece of vintage Americana that occasionally triggers and turns itself on automatically without anyone touching it. To the longtime residents, it is a known quirk of the town's impossible electrical grid.

Notable Lore
The Ominous Seizure (Season 1): The Diner is the site of a terrifying supernatural manifestation. While observing the newly arrived Matthews family during dinner, words painfully carve themselves into Sara Myers’ arm: "KILL THE BOY." Immediately following the message, Sara collapses into a severe seizure, signaling her deep and forced connection to the entities.

Victor's Warning (Season 1): During a community breakfast, Victor approaches Ethan Matthews inside the Diner to warn him that the forest trees are moving four inches closer to the town. He also identifies Ethan's "Boy in White" from a childhood sketch, confirming to himself that the entity has returned.

The Shift in Grief (Season 3): Following the brutal murder of Tian-Chen Liu by the Creatures, the Diner shifts into a state of mourning. Jade Herrera and Kristi Miller prepare her body at the church while the townspeople gather near the Diner to mourn the loss of the woman who sustained their daily survival.
