Tom’s Bar (The Distillery)
The Bar is a converted roadside service station that serves as the town’s primary social hub and moonshine distillery. Originally established and managed by Tom, a former philosophy professor, the building has shifted from a place of grim escapism into a makeshift research center. Since the town lacks commercial alcohol, the bar is famous for its "potato vodka," a harsh moonshine brewed in a rudimentary still in the back.

Recent Events and Current Status
The Bar is currently in a state of mourning and transition following the death of its owner. During the events surrounding the collapse of the Matthews' house, Tom was trapped in the wreckage while trying to help. He was ultimately killed by the monsters during the night, leaving the establishment without its original caretaker.

Since Tom's death, the location has been effectively commandeered by Jade Herrera. No longer just a place to drink, the bar has become Jade’s base of operations for his increasingly obsessive attempts to solve the town's puzzles.
